Quit Homelab Wizard and stop the homelab?
Your 34 apps go offline, on this Mac and on your phones, and nothing is backed up or updated until you open Homelab Wizard again (or log in again).
Quit stops everything, after one question
⌘Q in the window, or Quit Homelab Wizard… in the menu, asks this once. Quit and Stop closes the window and the menu bar item, stops the background agent, and stops every app.
Opening Homelab Wizard again starts them all, as does the next login.
On Windows it is the PC's own dialog with the same words (Alt+F4, or Quit in the tray menu); on Linux the desktop's.
