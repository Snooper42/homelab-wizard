KDE Plasma and SteamOS: the panel is at the bottom, so the menu opens upward
Click the icon in the system tray, beside the clock. Plasma draws the menu in Breeze: square-ish corners, its blue highlight, checkboxes in the left column. In SteamOS's desktop mode the rows are taller, for a controller or touch.
GNOME (Ubuntu's default) is the one with the bar at the top: there the same menu drops down from the top right, in GNOME's rounded panel style, and needs the AppIndicator extension, as Setup already says.
