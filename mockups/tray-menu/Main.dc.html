What changes
The first lines say how the homelab is, in one glance: a coloured dot, then only what needs you (an app restarting, a backup that did not run, updates waiting). Each opens the right place.
Open an app lists the apps by category with their state, instead of a plain list; one that needs a look says why in the menu.
Keep this Mac awake is a switch you can see, ticked while it is on. Quit says it stops the homelab, and asks once.
